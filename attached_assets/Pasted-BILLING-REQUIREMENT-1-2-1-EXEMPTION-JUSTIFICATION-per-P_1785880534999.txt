(1) vinyl manufacturing (optional);
(2) digital access units to our platform, my.goodtunes.music, which we host and operate — artists set fan-facing retail and keep the margin;
(3) GoodDeed printed certificates (optional), individually numbered;
(4) fulfillment/shipping (optional, flat pass-through).

Removing the app would not change what an artist buys from us or owes us.
