# GoodTunes for Artists

**Own the relationship with your fans, get the credits and gear you actually used in front of them, and earn — from your records, from your gear, and from the artists you bring on.**

*Dated May 29, 2026*

---

## What it is in one paragraph

GoodTunes is a fan-first player where fans buy your album directly from you (no streaming-service middleman), listen in an Apple-Music-quality experience, and discover the musicians, instruments, and brands behind every track. You get a real listening relationship with the people who buy your music, richer credits than any streaming service shows, a gear affiliate revenue stream on the equipment you used, and referral credit for every other artist you bring onto the platform.

## The problem with how artists ship music today

1. **Streaming pays pennies and hides everyone but the headliner.** The session players, the writers, the gear — invisible.
2. **You don't own your fan list.** The platform does. You can't talk to the people who actually listen.
3. **Your craft is uncredited.** The exact guitar, amp, mic, and the people who played them never reach the fan.
4. **There's no second revenue line.** Fans love the gear on your records, but there's no path from "what's that guitar?" to a sale you earn from.

## What works today

Everything below is live in production, not a slide:

- **The album plays the moment a fan pays** — streaming, leak-proof, on any device they sign in on. The master never leaves our infrastructure as a downloadable file (same delivery model used by HBO Max and Robinhood), so there's no street-date delay and no leak risk.
- **SuperCredits™** — per-track credits down to *which* 1973 Martin D-28 was used on a take, with every performer, instrument, and piece of gear surfaced and tappable. Fans can buy the gear through affiliate-attributed links, and the credit can travel even for a track GoodTunes doesn't host (it hands the fan off to their own streaming service to listen).
- **GoodSync™ synced lyrics** that scroll word-by-word with the song.
- **Per-song splits — publishing + master recording.** Each track carries its own writer and performer splits, so attribution and payouts match how the record was actually made.
- **Your own reporting dashboard.** A Stripe-grade `/artist` view: KPI strip (gross, your share, units, plays, unique listeners, top track, top album, completion rate) with prior-period comparison, daily revenue and plays charts, a buyers-and-listeners-by-country panel, top-tracks/top-albums tables, a reconciles-to-Stripe orders log, and an Audience tab with new-vs-returning, a repeat-listener cohort, and top fans. Every table exports to CSV.
- **A Sell panel that shows your economics before you commit.** Pick format, press, color, jacket, and quantity and see cost, profit, and total live — with an ⓘ tooltip breaking cost into Manufacturing + Publishing + Payment processing + GoodTunes margin. The signed-cert add-on shows a live "You earn $X.XX per unit" readout (mint when you profit, heart-pink when you'd lose money). Export the whole quote as a branded PDF to share.
- **GoodDeed collectibles + gifting.** Every copy fans buy gets a signed, numbered GoodDeed certificate (digital, with an optional printed-and-signed-by-you add-on). Fans can gift a single copy at a time with a message and a deliver-on date.
- **Bring other artists and get paid.** Refer an artist onto GoodTunes and earn a flat **$1.00 per paying unit** (adjustable) on their sales, tracked in a Referrals tab. Artist-to-artist invites are one-project with a built-in swap rule, and you can see the per-invitee funnel.
- **Edit your own release yourself — in the same tool the GoodTunes team uses.** Open one of your releases and change the details, credits and gear, lyrics, masters, and pricing directly, without emailing anyone. While a release is still being prepped, your edits go live instantly. Once it's out, an edit is sent to GoodTunes for a quick review before it changes the public page (you'll see "Sent for review") — the same after your first sale — so a record fans have already bought can't shift under them by accident. You never see the platform's internal cost math or any other artist's data.
- **You're invite-only and intentional.** There's no public artist self-signup; you land in the admin shell already wired to your profile and data.

## How it works

```
You design and price your release in the Sell panel
(format, press, color, quantity — economics shown live)
          ↓
Fans buy direct — album plays instantly, leak-proof,
with SuperCredits™ and synced lyrics
          ↓
Every copy mints a numbered GoodDeed; fans can gift copies
          ↓
You watch plays, buyers, top fans, and your share in your dashboard
          ↓
Fans buy the gear on your records → affiliate revenue
You bring other artists → $1/unit referral credit for one year
```

## How the money flows to you

| Flow | How it works |
|---|---|
| Your share of sales | Stripe Connect pays your artist share when an order is marked fulfilled; the orders log reconciles to Stripe. |
| Gear affiliate (SuperCredits™) | Tappable, affiliate-attributed gear links pay out when fans buy the equipment on your records. |
| Signed GoodDeed add-on | Optional printed-and-signed certificate, priced with a live per-unit profit readout. |
| Referral credit | $1.00 per paying unit (adjustable) on every artist you refer, for one year from when they join, rolled up by referred artist. |
| Release control | Every payout lands in a held queue and is released explicitly, so transfers are controlled and auditable. |
