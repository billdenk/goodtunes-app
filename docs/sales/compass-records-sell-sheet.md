**Turn the FLAC line nobody plays into a listening experience your fans actually use — and an audience you keep forever.**

## The problem with how digital ships today

Your current digital options at checkout:

| Format | Price | What the fan gets |
|---|---|---|
| MP3/320 Pre-Order | $8.00 | A download link on street date |
| FLAC Pre-Order | $9.60 | A download link on street date |
| WAV Pre-Order | $9.60 | A download link on street date |
| WAV-HD Pre-Order | $13.60 | A download link on street date |
| CD Pre-Order | $15.00 | A physical disc, plus the same download |

Three things every label has been quietly tolerating:

1. **Delivery is delayed to street date** to prevent leaks. The fan paid, but waits — and you've lost the moment of excitement.
2. **Most fans don't know what to do with FLAC or WAV files.** They get the email, click the link, and the files end up in a Downloads folder no one ever opens.
3. **You sold them once and the relationship ends at the download link.** No play data, no list of who actually listened, no path to the next album.

## What GoodTunes does instead

Same line in your Shopify checkout, completely different experience for the fan:

- **The album plays *the moment they pay.*** Inside GoodTunes — streaming, leak-proof, on any device they sign in on. No street-date delay, because the master never leaves our infrastructure as a downloadable file. Same delivery technology used by HBO Max and Robinhood.
- **No "what do I do with this?" moment.** The fan clicks one button in your order-confirmation email and is listening 15 seconds later.
- **A real listening experience.** Apple-Music-style player with the artist's exact 30-second preview window, synced lyrics that scroll with the song, and SuperCredits™ — per-track credits down to *which* 1973 Martin D-28 was used on a take, with tappable affiliate links if the fan wants the gear.
- **A signed, numbered digital GoodDeed certificate** is included with every redemption — proof they were one of the first N people to own the record. Optional printed-and-signed-by-the-artist GoodDeed add-on is available at checkout, priced however you like.
- **You see who's listening.** GoodTunes hands you a per-release dashboard: redemptions, top-played songs, plays per fan, top fans by completion. The fan you sold to is already in your Shopify customer list — what we add is the engagement layer underneath, which is what tells you *which* fans to talk to about the next release.

## How it works

```
Fan checks out on compassrecords.com (Shopify)
          ↓
Shopify confirmation email + order-status page show
"Get your music now" button
          ↓
Fan lands on a Compass-branded GoodTunes redeem page —
email already filled, name already known
          ↓
Sets a password (or one tap with Google / Apple)
          ↓
Album plays. GoodDeed number assigned. Done.
```

For Compass, the change is one line item in Shopify and a paste-in install link. **No plugin to download. No developer needed.** Refunds in Shopify reverse the unlock automatically.
