# GoodTunes® — Capabilities

GoodTunes® is a fan-first music player built around the idea that a song is a **structured object** — audio, lyrics, credits, and the gear behind it — not just an MP3. Fans buy albums directly from the artist (no streaming-service middleman), listen in an Apple-Music-quality player on web and native, and discover the musicians, instruments, and brands behind every track. Artists get richer credits, a real listening relationship with their fans, and a new affiliate revenue stream through the gear they actually use.

A living, investor-facing catalog of what GoodTunes has **actually shipped**. Read this when you need to speak to investors, partners, or labels about the product as it exists today — not what's on the roadmap. For future-phase plans and deep-dives, see [`roadmap.md`](./roadmap.md).

This catalog reads in two lenses:

- **[For fans](#for-fans)** — what fans can do in the player today.
- **[For the operator / investor](#for-the-operator--investor)** — the admin tooling and operational process that lets a tiny team run the whole business. This is the "look how much leverage one operator has" story: how a release goes from a single pasted link to a streaming, leak-proof, fully-credited, fully-priced product for sale — in minutes, without a developer.

# For fans

What fans actually see and do in the player today — buy direct from the artist, listen in an Apple-Music-quality experience, and discover the people and gear behind every track.

## Listening, search & library

Three lenses on one library, fast search, and the same Apple-Music chrome on phone, tablet, desktop, and native iOS/Android.

- **Native iOS + Android shell, store-ready.** GoodTunes ships as a Capacitor-wrapped iOS and Android app from the same web codebase — bundle id `fm.goodtunes.player`, dark splash + status bar in the brand palette, on-device song downloads so albums play offline, and a single platform layer (`client/src/lib/platform.ts`) that hides the Chat tab on native and the **Buy** affordances on iOS so the app conforms to App Store guideline 3.1.1 (purchases happen on the web). The full submission runbook — privacy nutrition labels, demo-account flow, deep-link verification, and reviewer notes — lives in [`app-store-submission.md`](./app-store-submission.md); the build mechanics live in [`native-builds.md`](./native-builds.md).
- **View Albums / Songs / Artists.** Browse the library through three lenses on the same catalog, with album art and artist photos inline.
- **Search and sort.** Fast search across albums, songs, and artists from a single field at the top of the library, with sort by artist or title.
- **Shuffle.** One-tap shuffle on any album, playlist, or auto-collection.
- **Favorite.** Heart a song or star an artist. Favorites are saved to the fan's GoodTunes account on the server, so they survive logout and follow the fan to a new phone or browser. Favorited artists' songs roll into the auto-built Favorites playlist (see below).
- **Playlists, including an auto-playlist from favorites.** Fans build their own playlists, and a virtual "Favorites" playlist is maintained automatically — favorited songs plus every song by a favorited artist, deduped, freshest first.
- **Play Next / Play Last.** Queue any song to play immediately after the current track, or drop it at the end of the queue.
- **AirPlay to Apple TV / HomePod.** On iPhone, when an AirPlay target is nearby the player shows Apple's real AirPlay control; one tap opens the native iOS device picker and routes the currently-playing audio to the chosen speaker or TV. The button only appears when a target is actually available — so it never looks broken — and stays hidden on Android, desktop, and in-app browsers. Playback keeps using the same encrypted, signed stream, so masters still never leave as a file.
- **Tablet + desktop responsive storefront.** Library, Search, Recents, Playlists, Account, and artist pages now reflow for tablet (≥768px, 3-column grids) and desktop (≥1024px, fixed left sidebar nav + 4–6 column grids, Apple-Music-web-style chrome) instead of stretching the 390px mobile layout. The floating bottom-nav pill stays on phones; desktop swaps in a left sidebar with Library / Search / Recents / Playlists, your playlists, and an account footer. Native iOS/Android (Capacitor) always stays on the mobile shell. The Mini Player anchors bottom-right of the content area at desktop widths.
- **Apple-Music-density desktop album page.** On laptop and desktop, every album opens into a full-width fan surface with a 280-pixel cover hero, artist + meta + description, and an Apple-Music-style track list — rose play triangle on hover, animated rose equalizer on the currently-playing row, hover-reveal "add to playlist" chip, and a tightened bottom Player dock sized to Apple's proportions. A right-side lyrics panel slides in from the dock's lyrics button. The hero CTAs adapt to ownership: owners see **Play + Shuffle**; non-owners see a **rose-outline Play pill** that auditions 30-second previews of each track and auto-advances through the album (with a "Preview" badge and a 30-second-capped scrubber in the bottom dock), next to a **rose-filled Buy pill** that shows the bundle price at rest, swaps to "Buy Now" on hover, and launches checkout — the same per-row hover triangle also fires a 30-second preview, and a copy-link button next to the CTAs gives fans and artists a shareable album URL they can paste into socials, email, or DMs. Hovering the Buy pill (or tapping on a touch surface) reveals an **Add signed GoodDeed +$X** chip the fan can toggle in before checkout, which hands the selection through to the Buy sheet with the add-on pre-checked. When a preview session walks the entire album to its end, the Buy sheet auto-opens so the moment closes with a clear CTA instead of dead silence. The mobile-web album page now matches this preview-first behavior — on Safari / Chrome / Android web, a not-yet-owned album plays as a 30-second-per-track preview session that ends in the same Buy prompt, while iOS native keeps the in-app player behavior since IAP isn't wired. The same primitive renders the admin album preview pane so editors see exactly what fans see.
- **Encrypted adaptive streaming with per-session tokens.** Masters never leave our infrastructure as a downloadable file. Each play request mints a short-lived, user-bound token, and audio is delivered as encrypted adaptive-bitrate segments — Spotify-Web-grade leak resistance, while letting fans listen instantly on any connection.
- **Apple-style split nav + unified search across the player.** The fan-shell bottom nav now reads as a labeled three-tab pillow on the left (Collection · Playlists · Recents) plus a standalone search circle on the right — the same shape Apple Music's player wears, with the search control sized to match the tab pillow's height and a clean gap between them. The Chat tab is gone from the dock; its unread count folds into a small red dot on the new account avatar in the Collection top-right (one tap into /account). On phones the search circle now expands leftward in place into a search field right inside the dock (tap again to collapse) — focusing the input and raising the keyboard in the same tap, so the keyboard reliably appears on iOS — and surfaces Recently Searched on open. Search is a first-class destination: typing fans out a ranked mixed result list (Top Results, Artists, Albums, Songs, Bonus Content, Gear, Vendors, Labels, People, Playlists) with horizontal Apple-style category pills for drilling in, a Recently Searched chip rail on landing, and per-fan history that survives logout. A new server-backed **Recents** tab groups everything the fan opened — albums, songs, artists, gear — into Today / Yesterday / This Week / Earlier, with swipe-to-remove and Clear All, capped at 200 entries.
- **One-app shell across every fan surface.** Artist pages, album pages, the vendor sheet, and chat all wear the same Apple-Music-grade chrome — 44-pixel circular icon buttons (Apple-HIG minimum), a single shared bottom-nav clearance so content never slides under the floating nav and mini-player, and a steadier scroll-hide that no longer flickers on small momentum scrolls. Ready for the upcoming Capacitor wrap so the App Store build looks intentional from screen one.

## The song as a structured object — credits, gear & lyrics

What makes GoodTunes more than a player: every track carries synced lyrics, per-performer credits, the exact gear used, and tappable links to buy it.

- **"Signed to {Label}" link on the artist page → fan label page.** Every fan-facing artist page now shows a small Apple-Music-style "Signed to {Label Name}" subhead above the artist's name when that artist is signed to a label, tappable straight through to a new fan label page at `/label/:id`. The label page shows the label's logo, name, and a grid of every GoodTunes release on that label (excluding hidden, prepping, and soft-deleted rows + streaming-only rows that aren't real GoodTunes releases). The existing `/label` partner dashboard route stays untouched.
- **Dynamic cross-linking across the catalog.** Every Vendor, Artist, Gear item, and Label is a first-class entity that links to every other. Tap a guitar inside a song's credits and you land on its vendor's page; tap the vendor and you see every artist on GoodTunes who plays their gear. The catalog acts like a graph, not a list of files.
- **Preview slider.** Every track carries a precision-trimmed 30-second preview window the artist sets visually against the waveform. Fans hear the artist's chosen hook anywhere a preview plays — search, share cards, free-tier teasers — instead of a hard-cut first 30 seconds.
- **GoodSync™ synced lyrics.** Lyrics scroll in time with the music in the Apple-Music-style overlay, with the active line in focus and surrounding lines softly blurred. Tapping any line seeks the song to that lyric — fans navigate the song by what's being sung, not by a timecode. The auto-sync workflow runs against any master length — when the master is too large for the forced-alignment provider's own size cap, GoodTunes transcodes a throwaway alignment-only copy on the fly so cues come back from any song, not just the short ones.
- **Chorus finder.** GoodTunes can identify a song's chorus from its lyric structure and audio and offer to snap the 30-second preview window to it, so previews and share cards default to the part of the song that hooks people fastest.
- **SuperCredits™.** Per-track credits that go far beyond Apple's writer-only list — every performer, the specific instrument they played on that take (down to "1973 Martin D-28"), tuning notes, and tappable affiliate links to buy the gear. Artists keep the lion's share of any sale. Credits turn into a real revenue stream, and a fan-discovery surface no other player offers.
- **Album-wide production credits.** Every album page (fan + admin) renders a grouped Produced / Mixed / Mastered / Engineered panel above the tracklist — each credited person is an avatar + inline-linked name that opens the same Person sheet song-level credits already use, free-text-only rows render as plain text, and the panel collapses past six role rows behind a Show-all toggle so long liner-notes don't bury the music.
- **Every credited person is tappable, everywhere.** Wherever a person appears in credits — song-level SuperCredits™, the album-wide production panel, the desktop album view, and the admin credits panel — anyone with a real record is an Apple-Music-style tappable link to their Person sheet (About / Music / Gear), while people who are just free-text liner-notes stay plain text. Production-only roles that never touch a track (Mastered by, A&R, …) resolve too: their sheet opens on About with the credited role as the subtitle instead of dead-ending.
- **Gear bookmarks.** Fans can bookmark any piece of gear they spot inside SuperCredits™ — the guitar they noticed on track 3 lives in a dedicated Bookmarks page in their account, one tap away later.
- **Vendor chat (demo).** A Chat tab in the player lets a fan open a thread with the vendor behind any piece of gear directly from its credit row — the instrument is auto-attached as a preview card so the conversation starts in context. Today this is a working demo of the UX (threads live on-device, replies are canned); real vendor accounts and live routing are scoped for the next phase.
- **Stream-elsewhere credited tracks.** GoodTunes can carry the full SuperCredits™ for a track it doesn't host — every writer, performer, instrument, and gear link — and hand the fan off to their own streaming service to actually listen. On an album that's earned the SuperCredits™ badge, each non-hosted track is tappable straight to the fan's service and the big control reads "Stream this"; an album with no credits gets a single "open the whole album on Spotify" handoff and no badge. The first handoff asks which service the fan prefers — Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Qobuz, Deezer, or Pandora — then remembers it — saved to their account so it follows them across devices, and changeable any time from Account → Settings → Streaming Service. Operators paste each service's album/artist URL into its own labeled field in the admin editor. Masters we host still never leave as a file; a track we never hosted simply opens where it lives.
- **All six services on the artist-page "How to Play" sheet.** Tapping a release on an artist page opens an Apple-Music-style sheet that now lists all six streaming services — Apple Music, Spotify, Tidal, Qobuz, Deezer, and Pandora — instead of just Apple Music + Spotify. Each row uses the release's stored deep link when one exists and otherwise falls back to a per-service search for the artist + title, so a fan can always hand off to whichever service they use. Apple Music and Spotify keep their official app icons; the other four show recognizable brand marks drawn from the same shared glyph set as the in-album picker.

## Buying, owning & gifting

Buy direct from the artist, get a numbered GoodDeed collectible, share or gift it, and track physical fulfillment — all leak-proof.

- **Legacy gogoods.com GoodDeed certificates regenerated.** Every imported gogoods.com owner gets a fresh, GoodTunes-branded GoodDeed certificate PDF — keyed off the original certificate number so #042 stays #042 — downloadable from the Library "Download GoodDeed" action on each imported copy. Paper size auto-picks Letter or A4 from the fan's shipping country, and the admin print queue tags these as "Legacy" so operators don't accidentally re-print certs fans already physically own.
- **GoodDeed sharing.** Fans can share a song by name and username over social and email with a branded GoodDeed certificate — every share carries the fan's identity, the unique GoodDeed serial number for that copy of the album, and a record of the purchase. Beyond the link share, fans **save the actual card as a high-res PNG** straight from the mobile certificate view in the format that fits where they're posting: a **Square / Portrait / Story picker** reshapes the card between a **1:1 1080×1080** feed post, a **4:5 1080×1350** portrait feed post (the format Instagram/Facebook now default to), and an immersive **9:16 1080×1920** Instagram/TikTok story. All three share one signature — a GoodTunes-orange edge-to-edge frame, album art fading into navy, the fan's photo straddling the seam, then "This GoodDeed® certifies → name → [GoodTunes | #NN] pill → album caption" (square + portrait use square feed corners, the story floats with a rounded curve). Saving snapshots an off-screen full-resolution stage so the exported PNG is always exactly sized, honoring the selected identity (Display / @username / Real Name) and the fan's owner photo, then opens the native share sheet (Save to Photos, post to Instagram/Stories) where supported and falls back to a direct download otherwise. And when a fan shares the **link**, it now unfurls with a real 1200×630 preview image rendered server-side that shows the actual GoodDeed card — album art, the fan's name, and the serial number — instead of a generic thumbnail (off-host art URLs are rejected so the image renderer can't be used to probe internal endpoints).
- **Real link previews.** Every shareable GoodTunes URL unfurls in iMessage, Slack, Twitter, WhatsApp, and email with a proper preview card — albums show the real cover, title, and artist; artist pages show the artist photo and name; gear pages show the instrument and its maker; the app root and any unmatched route fall back to a branded GoodTunes card on the dark-navy brand. Admin URLs render the same neutral card with `noindex` so a mis-pasted admin link never leaks a record title or thumbnail.
- **Locked GoodDeed print template + permanent name confirm.** Every printed GoodDeed — whether a fan downloads the single-page PDF from their Orders page or the press receives the multipage batch from the admin Sale-window panel — flows through one server-side template aligned to the approved Figma spec (Letter 8.5×11" with a white mat on all four sides, square album art filling the mat width, navy footer band with circular artist thumb, GoodTunes logo, QR + "GoodDeed®" caption, certifying paragraph, and CEO signature), so the bytes the fan unwraps match the bytes the press printed. Sequence-number uniqueness is guaranteed at the database (partial unique index on `(album_id, good_deed_number)`) with a retry loop wrapping every mint, so a concurrent webhook race can never put two fans on the same number. The fan's name-on-cert picker now warns "this is printed permanently" and locks on first submit — server rejects any later edit — so what the fan confirms is what ships.
- **Printable, signed GoodDeed certificates.** When a fan adds the signed-certificate option to an album order, GoodTunes builds a print-ready PDF for them — album art, the unique GoodDeed serial number, Nick's signature, a QR code that resolves to a per-deed provenance page on the open web, and the recipient name the fan picks at confirmation (their display name, username, or real name, same chooser as the digital share). Paper size auto-picks from the shipping country — US/Canada/Mexico get US Letter, everywhere else gets A4 — with a per-order admin override. An admin Print Queue page batches awaiting and confirmed certificates into a single ZIP (or merged PDF) for the printer, flips the rows to "printed" on download, and the QR target stays live for fans and gift recipients to verify the deed afterward. On the fan's Orders page, the signed-cert row carries a live status pill that walks through awaiting → queued for next print run → locked for printing → printed & on its way, and once printed a "View certificate" link drops them straight onto the public provenance page — so they're never wondering where their certificate is.
- **GoodDeed gifting — per-copy, scheduled, with a message.** Any paid order can be turned into a gift with a shareable claim link, and multi-quantity orders now gift a *single copy* at a time so a fan who bought four can send one to each of four people. Senders attach an optional 500-character message (rendered on the recipient's claim page on a brand-heart card) and an optional deliver-on date — the link stays "unlocks on X" until a daily scheduler tick stamps it live, so birthday gifts actually arrive on the birthday. At creation, GoodTunes resolves the recipient's email or phone against existing accounts and reserves the entitlement for that account so a stranger with the URL can't claim it. The gifting window is operator-configurable (default 30 days post-purchase). When a sender claim arrives, GoodTunes logs a sender-side confirmation; if the buyer refunds before the gift is claimed, the share link is auto-cancelled and the entitlement stays with the sender.
- **Orders page.** Fans see every album they've purchased, their GoodDeed numbers, and the status of any physical product (vinyl, merch) in one place. Each order row carries a fulfillment pill (Awaiting fulfillment → Submitted → In fulfillment → Shipped → Delivered) driven by OrderDesk, plus an inline carrier + tap-to-track link once the package is moving, and expands to a detail sheet with line items, shipping address, the full status timeline with timestamps, and a gift sender/recipient block. Every vinyl line item — on the Buy sheet's "You'll get" preview, the /welcome post-checkout receipt, on each Orders row + detail item, and now on the admin fulfillment queue at `/admin/orders` (row thumbnail + a per-vinyl-line block with color name, jacket upgrade, and quantity) — renders the album jacket with the colored disc the artist actually picked (35-swatch palette, gatefold-aware), so the record on the fan's screen is the record that arrives, and Bill can scan the queue at a glance without opening every order. The Account profile surfaces a "My Orders" card with the count and a one-line latest-status line that deep-links straight in.
- **Multi-copy checkout with per-copy signed certificates.** The web Buy sheet now lets fans pick a quantity (1–10, capped by remaining stock) and toggle the signed-GoodDeed add-on on each individual copy — so a fan can grab two copies of the same record where only one is signed (e.g. one to keep, one to gift). The live breakdown shows the format line × N plus the add-on line × K so the math is obvious before checkout, Stripe receives the correct per-line quantities, and each paid copy materializes as its own entitlement carrying its own GoodDeed number. The post-checkout receipt lists every copy by position with its serial number, and refunds void every per-copy serial and restore stock by the full quantity. iOS web continues to be gated behind the existing IAP rule.

## Account, onboarding & returning fans

One-tap sign-in, a clean first-run, and a warm welcome back for fans we've seen before.

- **"We saved your seat" welcome-back sheet.** Recognized returning fans get an Apple-Music-style full-screen sheet on their first launch after each app update, recapping what's new since they were last in (signed GoodDeeds, GoodSync™ lyrics, vendor chat) and pointing them straight back to their collection. Dismissals are stamped per-version on the fan's account, so the sheet only re-appears when GoodTunes ships a new wave — and brand-new sign-ups fall through to the new-fan onboarding flow instead.
- **Dual sign-in shells, one product.** Fans and operators sign in through completely separate accounts on `my.goodtunes.music` and `admin.goodtunes.music` — the same human can hold both with the same email without the two ever colliding. Admin sign-in always requires a second factor (one-time code by email by default, with an option to switch to an authenticator app).
- **One-screen finish-signup for Apple & Google fans.** A fan who signs up with Apple or Google completes a single Apple-Music-styled screen before they land in the player — pick a unique @handle (vetted live against a reserved-artists list so a random fan can't grab `@taylorswift`), confirm the display name other fans will see, and (only when Apple's Hide-My-Email relay is the only address we have) add a deliverable contact email or phone so receipts, gift notifications, and shipping updates actually reach them. Password signups skip the screen because they already supplied all three up-front.
- **Usernames.** Every fan picks a public username at sign-up that travels with their shares, playlists, and (soon) listening insights.
- **Profile photos with Google avatar capture.** Fans and partners can upload a profile photo from Edit Profile (PNG/JPEG/WebP/GIF, 5 MB cap, magic-byte validated) and the bytes land in Object Storage alongside album art and vendor logos — no more 5 MB base64 blobs round-tripping through Postgres on every profile load. When a brand-new fan signs in with Google for the first time, GoodTunes pulls the avatar Google already returned and writes it to the same storage path, so their first sight of `/account` isn't a blank camera glyph. Returning Google users keep whatever they've uploaded — the auto-grab is first-login-only.
- **Welcome-back flow for imported fans.** Every verified gogoods.com fan whose row carries `legacy_gogoods_id` gets a one-tap email-link sign-in — no password, no re-signup. The admin "Welcome back" surface lists the audience (imported / eligible / already mailed / onboarded / merged), supports a mandatory dry-run before a live send, batches the wave 25-at-a-time with a 1-second cooldown between batches, and respects a `WELCOME_BACK_KILL_SWITCH` env-flag stop. Every send is logged per-recipient so an operator can retry only the failures. The link drops the fan into a 3-screen onboarding (pick a handle, confirm display + private real name, see their migrated library) and then hands them off to `/collection`. Fans who didn't get the mail can self-serve from a dismissible banner on the customer login page that posts to the same endpoint with constant-floor latency and non-enumerating responses. Apple "Hide my email" callbacks reattach the stable private-relay address back onto the matching imported row instead of creating a duplicate. Fans who already signed up on the new player before discovering their import can absorb the other account with a "These two accounts are me" affordance on their profile — they enter the other email, a 24-hour confirmation link is sent there, and tapping it reparents the losing row's user_albums + orders + playlists onto the surviving account, revokes every outstanding token on the losing row, and stamps a fan-visible "all in one place" recap. Admins see every absorbed account on the surviving customer's detail page with what moved and from where.
- **Phone verification (verify-once, reuse-everywhere).** The first time a fan sends a gift, the first time a partner opens payout settings, or any future account-recovery moment, GoodTunes texts a one-time 6-digit code via Twilio and stamps the user as phone-verified — every later high-trust action on the same account reuses that proof, so fans never enter their number twice. Rate-limited per phone and per IP, codes expire in ten minutes and survive at most five wrong guesses; the OTP itself is never logged. Admins see the verified phone (and the date it was confirmed) next to the verified-email badge on every customer detail page.

# For the operator / investor

The leverage layer. Everything below is admin/CMS tooling and operational process that lets a very small team — often one person — run a record label, a vinyl brokerage, a fan-commerce platform, and a payouts operation at once. The throughline: a release goes from raw assets to a streaming, leak-proof, fully-priced product without a developer in the loop, and every dollar in and out is visible and controlled from one shell.

## Build the catalog in minutes, not hours

One operator ingests a full album — masters, lyrics, credits, bonus media — from a single pasted link, and onboards a new partner by pasting their website. The grunt work of cataloguing is automated away.

- **Master tech-spec readout in admin.** Every track's Master tile shows what the file actually is — format, sample rate, bit depth, channels, file size, and duration — so the operator can confirm at a glance that a hi-res WAV came through intact. When the upload pipeline transcodes a 24-bit master to FLAC for browser streaming, both lines surface side by side: what fans stream and what ships to press.
- **Dropbox album ingestion.** An operator pastes a single Dropbox folder link and GoodTunes pulls in the full album — audio masters, lyric files, and bonus images and videos — in one ingest pass. What used to be a multi-hour manual upload becomes a few minutes.
- **Per-track credits import from Dropbox.** Operators paste a Dropbox link (single liner-notes file or a folder of per-track docs) on any song's credits panel and GoodTunes fetches the right file by title, reads PDF / Word / text, and proposes writers, performers, and production rows pre-matched to existing People — ready for one click of review before saving.
- **Artist-profile creation from Search.** When an operator searches for an artist who isn't in the catalog yet, GoodTunes can create their profile in place — pulling biography, photo, and metadata from trusted music sources — and the new profile is immediately wired into albums, credits, and search.
- **Paste-a-URL prefill for People.** Every Add Person dialog — album-artist, admin, ambassador, contact, invited artist — accepts a single pasted URL (Apple Music, Spotify, Bandcamp, or any generic bio page) and prefills name, title, biography, photo, and social links from the page's structured data, rehosting the photo to GoodTunes' own storage. Apple and Spotify URLs route through the existing confirm + discography backfill so the streaming-row rules still apply; Bandcamp and generic bio pages commit a populated Person row directly. Email-only entries (admins, ambassadors) also get a Gravatar avatar fallback the moment they're created.
- **One role step for every Person add.** Adding or attaching a Person — whether from global People, an album's artist picker, or a partner's "Add admin / ambassador" panel — now flows through a single Apple-style role step that cleanly separates two things operators used to conflate: the **access role** being granted (a locked card that names exactly what access the grant confers) and the **creative credits** the person carries (a searchable, multi-select chip set: Artist, Producer, Writer, Performer, or any catalog credit, free-text add allowed). Picking "Artist" up front turns the row into a full artist record immediately, so the old "add as admin, then reopen and convert to artist" dead-end is gone. Each Person's profile shows the same picker, and the credits a person earns from real track and album work roll up onto it automatically as read-only tags alongside the hand-set ones.
- **Auto-proposed lineups from SuperCredits™.** Whenever per-track credits land (Dropbox liner-notes import or paste-a-URL track importer), GoodTunes rolls the distinct performers across the album's tracks into a proposed lineup with role hints and a track count per person. The Album Lineup panel surfaces a one-click "Use N from credits" CTA — and per-person "+ Name (3 tracks)" chips for cherry-picking — so the new lineup capability scales across the catalog without per-album hand-curation. Works for solo artists too: if their record's credits name session players, the panel appears with the proposal ready to accept.
- **Google Places address autocomplete in every admin address field.** Operators typing a vendor, maker, reseller, pressing-plant, label, or fulfillment-partner address (including the FP receiving-dock shipping address) now get live Google Places suggestions inline — pick one and the field fills in the cleaned, fully-formatted address in a single tap, so onboarding partners stops being a freeform typo trap. Keys live server-side only (admin-only proxy, per-burst session token billing) and the field gracefully falls back to plain text input when the key isn't configured.
- **Paste-a-URL entity creation.** To add a new vendor, gear product, label, pressing-plant manufacturer, fulfillment-warehouse partner, or non-profit partner, an operator pastes the company's website. GoodTunes scrapes the name, domain, logo, cover, bio, and (for plants) location automatically and warns if the entity is already in the catalog — onboarding a new partner takes seconds, and duplicates are caught at creation. Manufacturer, vendor, and label detail pages each carry a one-tap "Refresh from website" action that re-runs the same scrape against the saved URL and only overwrites scraped fields that came back non-null, so logos, covers, and bios stay current when a partner redesigns its site without clobbering hand-curated specialties or contact info.
- **NPO directory with contacts.** Non-profit partners are now first-class in the admin shell. From `/admin/non-profits`, an operator clicks **Add NPO**, pastes the org's main website, and GoodTunes pulls the name, logo, and homepage automatically — landing the operator on the NPO's detail page where they attach one or more People (with optional role labels like "Director" or "Program Lead") as contacts/reps. The same page surfaces the NPO's referral summary so operators can see attributed paid units and accrued $1/unit credits alongside who actually represents the org.
- **Shared contacts surface on every partner.** The same People-as-contacts panel is now wired into every partner type — vendors (makers + resellers), pressing plants, labels, fulfillment partners, and NPOs — so operators can record the buyer, A&R, production lead, or account rep on whichever org they're working with. Each panel ships with a "Paste LinkedIn" add-flow that reads the public profile (name, headline, avatar), rehosts the avatar, and find-or-creates the underlying Person matched by their canonical LinkedIn URL — a single paste turns into a saved contact, with a graceful fallback when LinkedIn blocks the scrape so the link still gets captured.

Approving re-checks live eligibility (a refund draining the pool or a revoked consent blocks it), stamps the cut as triggered, writes a `release` ledger row sized to the floor, and mints the Stripe Connect earmark that pays the press at the next payout cycle. The Sell panel and press page both show a live pool readout (accrued / released / available vs. floor) so the artist and operator watch the run fund itself in real time, and a "pool ready" chip lights up on the press Pipeline card the moment a release qualifies.

Every format row now shows the same Price · Cost · Profit / Sold · Profit · Total two-column readout the signed-cert add-on uses, with an ⓘ tooltip breaking Cost into Manufacturing + Publishing + Payment processing + GoodTunes margin so artists can see exactly where the unit cost comes from before they price the run. The card autosaves on field change (no Save button), the cover-art pencil now lives inside the album jacket itself, and every dollar figure in the cost breakdown is rendered with thousands separators (`$1,234.56`) from `Intl.NumberFormat` so long runs read cleanly. The Manufacturing line stays sourced from the press catalog ladder; Publishing is computed live as `trackCount × $0.257`, Payment processing as `2.9% + $0.30`, and GoodTunes margin as a flat `$4.50` per unit. The Sell panel also opens with a Presses directory — info cards for every pressing plant GoodTunes works with (logo, location, turnaround, specialty chips) — and the footnote tells artists today's vinyl Cost defaults are Hellbender's reference rates. - **Vendor-managed GoodDeed pricing portal.** Pressing plants, printers, and holographers each get their own sign-in (role=vendor) on a dedicated `/vendor` portal where they quote per-leg GoodDeed costs themselves — Printing on a quantity-keyed price ladder, Hologram+shrinkwrap as a flat per-unit, and Insertion as a flat per-unit for vendors that also press. Each leg toggles Active/Draft independently. Print partners that only run paper (Hoover Printing is the seed) carry a separate **Quickprinter** capability — print-only, mutually exclusive with Maker — and quote their per-unit cost across a fixed rung set (50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900, 1000) per paper size; Letter is live today and 12×18 is scaffolded for the next large-format cert task. Per-leg printer / hologram / insertion routing is set **once** at the platform level on the Platform Pricing page (GoodDeed routing defaults), and every album's Sell panel resolves its live cost against those defaults instead of re-picking the same three vendors per release. When the sale window closes the per-release pricing is snapshotted onto the addon so later vendor edits never rewrite history.
- **At-a-glance GoodDeed pricing summary for every admin.** A read-only **GoodDeed pricing** page in the admin shell pulls every GoodDeed cost matrix — previously editable only on the super-admin Platform Pricing page — into one scannable surface any admin role can open: the signed-cert wholesale ladder (with the 25-unit minimum note), the routing-default Quickprinter's per-unit ladder (US Letter live, 12×18 coming soon), the flat hologram+shrinkwrap and insertion legs for the routing-default vendors, and a resolved per-cert total with a run-size input that recomputes the live vendor cost stack, the resolved wholesale rung, GoodTunes margin, and the per-run totals on the fly. Every dollar figure renders with thousands separators. The page never writes — editors get a deep link out to Platform Pricing.
- **Signed-cert sale-window batch workflow.** Every signed-cert release runs on a defined sale window inside the album's Sell panel — operators set the open and close dates, fan orders that land in-window reserve a GoodDeed number, and a live counter shows progress against the 25-unit print minimum. At close (manual or auto-tick), GoodTunes either auto-refunds every signed-cert line if the window finished under 25 (fans keep the digital provenance page) or snapshots the vendor pricing onto the release and flips the batch into production. From there a single "Send to press" button bundles the in-batch certificates into a ZIP for the printer, and a six-step ops tracker (sent to printer → printed → signed → hologrammed → shrinkwrapped → inserted) walks the run to shipped with timestamps recorded on each step. Post-window orders fall back to digital-only automatically, and a true-up ledger row records the projected-vs-actual rung delta at close so the next batch's economics start from the same source of truth.
- **Signed-cert wholesale is published, tiered, and god-view editable.** GoodTunes's per-unit wholesale price for printed, signed, hologrammed GoodDeed certificates is a tiered ladder (default 25–49 = $13, 50–99 = $12, 100–199 = $9, 200–299 = $7, 300+ = $6) with a 25-unit minimum (smaller runs auto-refund) and billing on actual orders at window close. Super-admin can now edit each rung — add or remove rungs (up to 10), rename the batch label, and change the wholesale per unit — directly inside the admin Platform Pricing page; the first rung stays pinned to the 25-unit print floor so the auto-refund rule can't be edited around. The live ladder powers the Push-to-Shopify earnings preview and the future window-close auto-charge from the same source of truth, so operators always quote the same number the system bills. See [docs/shopify-pricing-strategy.md](./shopify-pricing-strategy.md#signed-cert-wholesale-ladder) for the full cost stack.
- **Platform pricing + artist profit readout.** A super-admin-only Platform Pricing page sets the wholesale cost of a printed & signed GoodDeed certificate and the per-order Shopify checkout fee. Every artist's signed-cert add-on shows a live "You earn $X.XX per unit" readout against that cost, in mint when they're profiting and in heart-pink when the price they've set would lose money. The platform cost is snapshot onto the add-on at save time, so raising the global cost never silently turns a saved artist add-on into a loss — they see the new number on their next save. Artists also pick a **planned quantity** for the signed run — either a fixed number (the Sell panel multiplies per-unit profit by the run to show a "Total if they all sell" number) or "as many as will sell" (Total shown as TBD). Fixed runs are a real soft cap on the fan-facing Buy sheet: once that many signed certificates have been bought across paid orders, the toggle disables with "All signed copies claimed" and the checkout endpoint rejects late attempts, so artists are never on the hook for more prints than they committed to.

## Onboard partners & grow the network

Invite trees, scoped permissions, and referral economics let the network grow itself — presses bring artists, NPOs bring artists, artists bring artists — each attribution wired to pay out automatically.

- **Non-profit invite tree — ambassadors, staff, artists in one shell.** Non-profit admins now run their own team from the NPO dashboard: invite ambassadors and staff onto a sub-role inside the NPO's scope, and invite artists who referrer-chain back to the NPO for the $1/unit credit. Ambassadors and staff can keep growing the artist roster from the same shell — they only see the "Invite an artist" CTA — and the NPO admin gets a "Team tree" tab showing who invited whom (NPO → ambassador/staff → artists, with Active vs Pending chips). All invite, resend, and revoke endpoints are scoped to one NPO id so a sub-role teammate can never reach into another non-profit's queue.
- **Artist-to-artist invites with referral chain + per-invitee funnel.** Verified artists invite other artists onto GoodTunes directly from their dashboard — capped at five outstanding per artist so the channel can't be flooded. Every accepted invite stamps the inviter as `referred_by_person_id` on the new artist's row, so the existing $1-per-paid-unit referral economics light up without any operator follow-up. The dashboard's Invite panel doubles as a drill-down: each row shows status (Invited → Joined / Revoked / Expired), the dates that moved it, and once joined the units sold + pending payout that invitee has accrued — rolled up into a one-line funnel summary at the top of the panel. Super-admin curates a separate "earmarked folks" list of names Bill personally wants onboarded; those surface in each artist's dashboard as one-tap invite chips, and the row drops off the suggestion list as soon as any artist sends the invite.
- **Team invites with Person resolution + claimed-Person review** — Super-admin can invite Identity / Manager / Team teammates on a specific artist Person, optionally attaching a pre-flighted album draft so the invitee lands straight in the editor. Identity invites for claimed People (linked login, Spotify artist, group, or shipped GoodTunes release) and any team invite from a non-super-admin to an unknown email are held in a review queue until approved — no email goes out before then. Per-(artist, user) God-View permission matrix lets super-admin pin individual verbs (edit metadata, edit credits + gear, upload masters, map Shopify, manage payouts, invite subusers) on or off for one teammate without changing the scope-wide defaults.
- **Partner invites + referral credits.** A single admin invite sheet onboards every partner role — super-admin, label, artist, manufacturer, fulfillment, and **non-profit** — over a 14-day magic-link, with a one-click resend that mints a fresh token and a soft-revoke that keeps the audit trail. - **Multi-level invite trees (ambassadors, artist swaps, press visibility).** Referrals now go deeper than one hop. A non-profit can promote any of its contact people into an **ambassador** (toggled per-person from the admin Permissions tab); the ambassador can then attribute artist invites, and the resulting credits flow to that ambassador while still rolling up to their NPO. **Presses** can refer artists at $0/unit and get a project-scoped Sell-panel view of the artists they brought in (no platform-wide directory leak). **Artist-to-artist** invites are one-project only with a built-in **swap** rule — either side can pre-elect the other for one of their projects from the Referrals tab, the album's first paid sale **freezes** the swap state, and a duplicate-in-subtree guard makes the operator confirm before sending a parallel attribution. A super-admin-only **invite-tree visualiser** at `/admin/invite-tree` renders the full subtree under any artist, NPO, or press with per-node paid-unit + pending-payout totals, and a single flag on `/admin/invites` lifts the NPO referral rate from $1.00 to $1.50 per unit when GoodTunes wants to fund a bigger charity bonus out of platform margin.
- **Press partner Referrals card.** The press portal's Overview tab now carries a Referrals card listing every artist the press has brought onto GoodTunes — each row deep-links into the artist's admin page and lists the invited projects underneath with per-album paid-unit counts, plus a header total so a press can see the pending referral credit accruing as their artists sell records.

## Payments & payouts

Every dollar that moves to a partner is computed, queued, and released under control — no money leaves without an explicit, idempotent, audited release.

- **Monthly referral-credit payouts.** Accrued `referral_credits` rows (artist ambassadors, NPO referrers, person referrers) no longer sit pending forever — a super-admin-only batched payout job groups every still-pending credit by payee, fires one Stripe Connect transfer per partner against the same Stripe Express account they linked through the Payouts panel (now wired on Artist, Label, **and Non-Profit** detail pages), and stamps each cleared credit with the Stripe transfer id + `paid_at` so the dashboard KPIs flip from Pending payout → Paid out honestly. The run is idempotent (per-batch Stripe idempotency keys hashed off the credit ids), exposes a dry-run preview, and surfaces the obvious skip reasons (partner hasn't linked Stripe yet, payouts not enabled) instead of silently leaving rows pending. The admin home now carries a super-admin-only "Referral payouts ready" card — total $ + payee count + blocked count at a glance, with a Preview dialog (per-payee breakdown, deep links to the partner page) and a one-click Run with confirm step and a paid/skipped/failed result toast, so an operator no longer has to curl the endpoints. Suitable for a monthly cron or admin-triggered cycle.
- **Press invoice earmarks to Stripe.** The moment a press uploads (or updates) an invoice on its Pipeline card, GoodTunes mints a Stripe Connect transfer to the press's connected account for the captured total — idempotent per album, so retries and re-uploads can't double-mint. "Billed outside the system" skips the transfer entirely. The press's Settings → Payouts subtab surfaces the Connect account state and a roll-up of every captured invoice with its variance vs the locked masters-prep quote (green ≤5%, amber 5–10%, pink >10%) and the per-invoice transfer status — so a press can see at a glance which jobs have already been earmarked and which are waiting on Stripe onboarding.
- **Bill-only payout release queue.** Every Stripe Connect transfer the platform would have fired (per-order artist/label royalty, press invoice payout, monthly referral-credit batch) now lands in a **held** earmark at `/admin/payouts-release` instead of moving money. Bill walks the queue and clicks Release on each row — that action is where the actual Stripe transfer fires, idempotency-keyed on the earmark id so a double-click can never double-pay. Hold-longer attaches a free-text note; Reject closes the earmark, reverts the source row (order → `skipped`, referral credits → `pending_payout`, supersedes prior press-invoice earmark), and demands a reason that's pinned to the audit log. Refunding an order before release auto-cancels its earmark so the queue can't show a phantom row for money the customer already got back. Every other super-admin sees the queue read-only. A daily digest email summarises every still-held row to Bill (subject + scannable list) with a 20-hour dedupe so a server restart can't double-mail.

## Security, access & data

Hardened admin access, self-serve recovery, and the legacy migration that brought the existing fan base across.

- **2FA-protected admin sign-in.** Every admin sign-in requires a second factor — by default a one-time code emailed to the admin, with an option to switch to an authenticator-app TOTP enrollment and scrypt-hashed recovery codes. Super-admin grant and revoke are gated to existing super-admins from inside the app. Admins can also rotate their own password from `/admin/security` — current password as proof, 8-character minimum, no DB ticket required (OAuth-only admins see the card in a disabled state with a short explanation). A **Forgot password?** link on `/admin/login` emails a single-use, 30-minute reset link that lets a locked-out admin pick a new password without operator help — the 2FA gate still fires before they reach the admin shell, so a stolen reset email alone isn't enough. Fans signing into the player at `/login` get the same self-serve recovery — a **Forgot password?** link on the dark customer chrome emails a single-use, 30-minute reset link against `customer_users`, OAuth-only fans (Google/Apple) are silently skipped, and the same non-enumerating neutral-200 + per-IP/per-email rate limit guarantees apply, so locked-out fans no longer have to email support.
- **Admin access guard + promote-from-customers.** A signed-in fan who lands on `admin.goodtunes.music` sees a branded "access not authorized" modal instead of a silent bounce, and every super-admin gets one email per requester (24h dedupe) so the "we'll be in touch" copy is honest. Super-admins can now promote any existing customer in place from the Admin → Customers row — same email, same password, no second account — into any partner role (super_admin / label / artist / manufacturer / fulfillment / non_profit / vendor + scope).

## Integrations

The third-party services GoodTunes already runs against in production.

- **Replit Object Storage.** Durable cloud storage for every operator-uploaded asset — album art, person photos, vendor logos and covers, scraped instrument images — served through stable public URLs that survive redeploys.
- **OpenAI.** Powers operator-facing AI tools across the admin — biography drafting, content suggestion, structured metadata extraction, and SuperCredits™ enrichment — so the catalog can be built quickly with the artist's voice intact.
- **ElevenLabs.** Voice-AI engine behind GoodTunes' lyric workflows — transcribes vocals from a master and aligns artist-supplied lyrics word-by-word, then diffs the two so the artist can confirm or correct any mismatches before publishing. Synced lyrics ship without weeks of manual timing.
- **Mux.** Encrypted, adaptive-bitrate streaming with per-session signed tokens — masters never leave our infrastructure as a downloadable file. Same delivery model used by HBO Max and Robinhood. New uploads ingest into Mux automatically, a startup sweep brings any pre-Mux masters into the pipeline, and the startup sweep also re-checks errored assets and persists Mux's failure reason. The fan player is Mux-only — if signing fails, playback stops (no raw-file fallback). Admins see a per-track Mux state pill on every album page plus a top-of-app banner if the pipeline is misconfigured or any masters are stuck. Errored tracks also surface their auto-retry state inline — "next auto-retry in Nm" while the backoff ladder is still climbing, "retry cap reached — re-upload needed" once it gives up — and a one-click "Retry now" resets the backoff and re-ingests immediately.
- **Spotify.** Used at the catalog level to enrich albums, artists, and tracks with the canonical metadata fans recognize — release dates, artwork, identifiers — without depending on Spotify for playback.
- **GitHub.** Source of truth for every line of GoodTunes code, with automated checks on every change — engineering velocity stays high while production stays safe.
- **OrderDesk.** Every paid physical order — vinyl, cassette, CD, bundled merch, whether bought directly through GoodTunes or bundled into a label's Shopify checkout — is handed off to OrderDesk the moment Stripe (or Shopify) confirms payment, with the album, artist, label, and SKU stamped on the carton so the right warehouse sees the right routing. OrderDesk signs status webhooks back to GoodTunes (submitted → in fulfillment → shipped → delivered, plus carrier and tracking number/URL), and operators see the full lifecycle on the admin order row with a one-click retry if a handoff ever fails.
- **Google Sign-In.** Fans and admins can sign in with their Google account — one tap, no password.
- **Apple Sign-In.** Apple ID is a first-class login option for fans on iOS-heavy devices. Fans land back in the player whether they picked "Share My Email" or "Hide My Email"; identity is anchored on Apple's stable subject claim (never the email), so the same fan always resolves to the same account on resign-in. When Apple returns a private-relay forwarder, a banner in Account prompts the fan to verify a real deliverable address — once they do, a later Google sign-in with that same real email lands in the same account instead of creating a duplicate. Apple's domain-verification file (`/.well-known/apple-developer-domain-association.txt`) is served from disk on both `my.` and `admin.` hosts so it survives redeploys.
- **Stripe.** Direct in-player checkout for album bundles (digital + optional vinyl, merch, add-ons) runs on Stripe Embedded Checkout. Webhook-driven order fulfillment unlocks the album the instant payment clears, assigns the buyer's GoodDeed serial number, and reverses cleanly on refund. Stripe Connect powers artist and label payouts when an order is marked fulfilled.
- **Label-explainable Shopify upload flow.** Every album's Shopify tab opens with a plain-English "How the Shopify path works" explainer (dismissible per user) and a content-readiness checklist that mirrors the direct-to-fan flow — cover art, track masters, 30-second previews, and bonus content — so a label sees exactly what's still missing without touring every tab. Each checklist row jumps to the same Tracks / Bonus / artwork surfaces the vinyl flow uses, so there's one set of uploaders, not a parallel Shopify-only stack. Once the album is pushed to Shopify, the tab generates a per-album HTML snippet (album id baked in) for the label to paste into the Shopify product description, with one-line "where do I paste this" instructions, alongside the existing order-confirmation Liquid block.
- **Shopify Bundle.** Labels and artists who already sell physical product on Shopify can bundle GoodTunes digital access into the same checkout. After the buyer pays on the label's store, our app unlocks the album for the email on the order, mints a one-time redemption code, and adds a "Get your music now" button to both the order-status page and the confirmation email. The fan lands on a branded redeem page that already knows their name, sets a password (or signs in if they already have GoodTunes), and drops straight into the player with their GoodDeed number assigned. Refunds reverse the unlock automatically. Operators connect a label's store from our admin in two clicks — no plugin to download — and each album's admin Shopify tab maps the physical product (or specific variant) to the digital release. For labels who don't yet have a Shopify product, the admin Shopify tab also offers a one-click "Push to Shopify as a draft" — it creates the product in the label's store as a draft (never auto-published) with a "GoodTunes Edition" variant priced at the bundle price and inventory-capped to the album's redemption cap, plus an optional "+ Signed printed GoodDeed" variant when the signed-cert add-on is enabled, with a live earnings preview against the published signed-cert wholesale ladder. Re-pushing detects label-side edits in Shopify (title, body, variant prices/inventory) and asks the operator to confirm before overwriting. Once an album is pushed, the Shopify tab also shows a **Live on Shopify** mirror — current retail price and lifetime units sold per variant (GoodTunes Edition + signed-cert, side-by-side when both exist), pulled live from the Shopify Admin API with a one-click "Refresh from Shopify" and a relative-time stamp so operators can answer "what's it priced at, how many moved?" without leaving the album panel. Variants deleted on Shopify show "Removed in Shopify" instead of erroring.
