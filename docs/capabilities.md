A living, investor-facing catalog of what GoodTunes has actually shipped. Read this when you need to speak to investors, partners, or labels about the product as it exists today — not what's on the roadmap. For future-phase plans and deep-dives, see [`roadmap.md`](./roadmap.md).

## What GoodTunes is

GoodTunes® is a fan-first music player built around the idea that a song is a **structured object** — audio, lyrics, credits, and the gear behind it — not just an MP3. Fans buy albums directly from the artist (no streaming-service middleman), listen in an Apple-Music-quality player on web and (soon) native, and discover the musicians, instruments, and brands behind every track. Artists get richer credits, a real listening relationship with their fans, and a new affiliate revenue stream through the gear they actually use.

## Platform capabilities

The things that make GoodTunes more than another player.

- **Dynamic cross-linking across the catalog.** Every Vendor, Artist, Gear item, and Label is a first-class entity that links to every other. Tap a guitar inside a song's credits and you land on its vendor's page; tap the vendor and you see every artist on GoodTunes who plays their gear. The catalog acts like a graph, not a list of files.
- **Dropbox album ingestion.** An operator pastes a single Dropbox folder link and GoodTunes pulls in the full album — audio masters, lyric files, and bonus images and videos — in one ingest pass. What used to be a multi-hour manual upload becomes a few minutes.
- **Preview slider.** Every track carries a precision-trimmed 30-second preview window the artist sets visually against the waveform. Fans hear the artist's chosen hook anywhere a preview plays — search, share cards, free-tier teasers — instead of a hard-cut first 30 seconds.
- **GoodSync™ synced lyrics.** Lyrics scroll in time with the music in the Apple-Music-style overlay, with the active line in focus and surrounding lines softly blurred. Tapping any line seeks the song to that lyric — fans navigate the song by what's being sung, not by a timecode. The auto-sync workflow runs against any master length — when the master is too large for the forced-alignment provider's own size cap, GoodTunes transcodes a throwaway alignment-only copy on the fly so cues come back from any song, not just the short ones.
- **Chorus finder.** GoodTunes can identify a song's chorus from its lyric structure and audio and offer to snap the 30-second preview window to it, so previews and share cards default to the part of the song that hooks people fastest.
- **SuperCredits™.** Per-track credits that go far beyond Apple's writer-only list — every performer, the specific instrument they played on that take (down to "1973 Martin D-28"), tuning notes, and tappable affiliate links to buy the gear. Artists keep the lion's share of any sale. Credits turn into a real revenue stream, and a fan-discovery surface no other player offers.
- **Per-track credits import from Dropbox.** Operators paste a Dropbox link (single liner-notes file or a folder of per-track docs) on any song's credits panel and GoodTunes fetches the right file by title, reads PDF / Word / text, and proposes writers, performers, and production rows pre-matched to existing People — ready for one click of review before saving.
- **Artist-profile creation from Search.** When an operator searches for an artist who isn't in the catalog yet, GoodTunes can create their profile in place — pulling biography, photo, and metadata from trusted music sources — and the new profile is immediately wired into albums, credits, and search.
- **Dual sign-in shells, one product.** Fans and operators sign in through completely separate accounts on `my.goodtunes.music` and `admin.goodtunes.music` — the same human can hold both with the same email without the two ever colliding. Admin sign-in always requires a second factor (one-time code by email by default, with an option to switch to an authenticator app).

## Player features

What fans actually see and do.

- **View Albums / Songs / Artists.** Browse the library through three lenses on the same catalog, with album art and artist photos inline.
- **Search and sort.** Fast search across albums, songs, and artists from a single field at the top of the library, with sort by artist or title.
- **Shuffle.** One-tap shuffle on any album, playlist, or auto-collection.
- **Favorite.** Heart a song or star an artist. Favorited artists' songs roll into the auto-built Favorites playlist (see below).
- **Playlists, including an auto-playlist from favorites.** Fans build their own playlists, and a virtual "Favorites" playlist is maintained automatically — favorited songs plus every song by a favorited artist, deduped, freshest first.
- **Gear bookmarks.** Fans can bookmark any piece of gear they spot inside SuperCredits™ — the guitar they noticed on track 3 lives in a dedicated Bookmarks page in their account, one tap away later.
- **Play Next / Play Last.** Queue any song to play immediately after the current track, or drop it at the end of the queue.
- **GoodDeed sharing.** Fans can share a song by name and username over social and email with a branded GoodDeed certificate — every share carries the fan's identity, the unique GoodDeed serial number for that copy of the album, and a record of the purchase.
- **Real link previews.** Every shareable GoodTunes URL unfurls in iMessage, Slack, Twitter, WhatsApp, and email with a proper preview card — albums show the real cover, title, and artist; artist pages show the artist photo and name; gear pages show the instrument and its maker; the app root and any unmatched route fall back to a branded GoodTunes card on the dark-navy brand. Admin URLs render the same neutral card with `noindex` so a mis-pasted admin link never leaks a record title or thumbnail.
- **Printable, signed GoodDeed certificates.** When a fan adds the signed-certificate option to an album order, GoodTunes builds a print-ready PDF for them — album art, the unique GoodDeed serial number, Nick's signature, a QR code that resolves to a per-deed provenance page on the open web, and the recipient name the fan picks at confirmation (their display name, username, or real name, same chooser as the digital share). Paper size auto-picks from the shipping country — US/Canada/Mexico get US Letter, everywhere else gets A4 — with a per-order admin override. An admin Print Queue page batches awaiting and confirmed certificates into a single ZIP (or merged PDF) for the printer, flips the rows to "printed" on download, and the QR target stays live for fans and gift recipients to verify the deed afterward. On the fan's Orders page, the signed-cert row carries a live status pill that walks through awaiting → queued for next print run → locked for printing → printed & on its way, and once printed a "View certificate" link drops them straight onto the public provenance page — so they're never wondering where their certificate is.
- **GoodDeed gifting.** Any paid order can be turned into a gift with a shareable claim link — the recipient opens the link, signs in or signs up, and the album drops into their library with their name on the GoodDeed certificate.
- **Usernames.** Every fan picks a public username at sign-up that travels with their shares, playlists, and (soon) listening insights.
- **Vendor chat (demo).** A Chat tab in the player lets a fan open a thread with the vendor behind any piece of gear directly from its credit row — the instrument is auto-attached as a preview card so the conversation starts in context. Today this is a working demo of the UX (threads live on-device, replies are canned); real vendor accounts and live routing are scoped for the next phase.
- **Orders page.** Fans see every album they've purchased, their GoodDeed numbers, and the status of any physical product (vinyl, merch) in one place. Each order row carries a fulfillment pill (Awaiting fulfillment → Submitted → In fulfillment → Shipped → Delivered) driven by OrderDesk, plus an inline carrier + tap-to-track link once the package is moving, and expands to a detail sheet with line items, shipping address, the full status timeline with timestamps, and a gift sender/recipient block. Every vinyl line item — on the Buy sheet's "You'll get" preview, the /welcome post-checkout receipt, on each Orders row + detail item, and now on the admin fulfillment queue at `/admin/orders` (row thumbnail + a per-vinyl-line block with color name, jacket upgrade, and quantity) — renders the album jacket with the colored disc the artist actually picked (35-swatch palette, gatefold-aware), so the record on the fan's screen is the record that arrives, and Bill can scan the queue at a glance without opening every order. The Account profile surfaces a "My Orders" card with the count and a one-line latest-status line that deep-links straight in.
- **Apple-Music-density desktop album page.** On laptop and desktop, every album opens into a full-width fan surface with a 280-pixel cover hero, artist + meta + description, and an Apple-Music-style track list — rose play triangle on hover, animated rose equalizer on the currently-playing row, hover-reveal "add to playlist" chip, and a tightened bottom Player dock sized to Apple's proportions. A right-side lyrics panel slides in from the dock's lyrics button. The hero CTAs adapt to ownership: owners see **Play + Shuffle**; non-owners see a **rose-outline Play pill** that auditions 30-second previews of each track and auto-advances through the album (with a "Preview" badge and a 30-second-capped scrubber in the bottom dock), next to a **rose-filled Buy pill** that shows the bundle price at rest, swaps to "Buy Now" on hover, and launches checkout — the same per-row hover triangle also fires a 30-second preview, and a copy-link button next to the CTAs gives fans and artists a shareable album URL they can paste into socials, email, or DMs. The same primitive renders the admin album preview pane so editors see exactly what fans see.
- **Encrypted adaptive streaming with per-session tokens.** Masters never leave our infrastructure as a downloadable file. Each play request mints a short-lived, user-bound token, and audio is delivered as encrypted adaptive-bitrate segments — Spotify-Web-grade leak resistance, while letting fans listen instantly on any connection.
- **One-app shell across every fan surface.** Artist pages, album pages, the vendor sheet, and chat all wear the same Apple-Music-grade chrome — 44-pixel circular icon buttons (Apple-HIG minimum), a single shared bottom-nav clearance so content never slides under the floating nav and mini-player, and a steadier scroll-hide that no longer flickers on small momentum scrolls. Ready for the upcoming Capacitor wrap so the App Store build looks intentional from screen one.

- **Partner invites + referral credits.** A single admin invite sheet onboards every partner role — super-admin, label, artist, manufacturer, fulfillment, and **non-profit** — over a 14-day magic-link, with a one-click resend that mints a fresh token and a soft-revoke that keeps the audit trail. Every invite can carry a **referrer** (artist or non-profit) and a free-form welcome note; when the new partner is an artist, the referrer is automatically wired onto their profile so the existing attribution machinery lights up immediately. From that point on, every paid order on a referred artist's album mints a **$1/unit credit** to the referrer (ledgered idempotently in `referral_credits`), surfaced as KPIs + per-artist rollups inside both the artist dashboard's Referrals tab and a dedicated `/non-profit` partner shell — making it easy for artists and non-profits to bring new artists to GoodTunes and get paid as those artists sell records.

## Admin / CMS

The operator surface that powers the catalog.

- **Artists.** Full CRUD for artist profiles — photo, biography (filtered for legal-issue and controversy content), and the credits and albums they appear on.
- **Bands & members + per-album lineups.** Any artist profile can be marked as a group (band, duo, trio, orchestra, choir, ensemble); the People admin gains a Members tab to curate the band's roster from existing Person rows, with per-member roles, joined/left years, and ordering. Each album whose primary artist is a group surfaces a Lineup panel on its admin Overview — one tap drops in the band's current roster, and the operator can add/remove/reorder to capture the actual session lineup that played on that record. Fan-side ArtistDetail renders the current roster (and a "former members" disclosure) as an Apple-Music-density avatar rail, and AlbumDetail renders the per-album lineup directly under the bonus content — so a fan landing on a Steely Dan or Wrecking Crew record sees who actually played on it, not just the headline artist.
- **Auto-proposed lineups from SuperCredits™.** Whenever per-track credits land (Dropbox liner-notes import or paste-a-URL track importer), GoodTunes rolls the distinct performers across the album's tracks into a proposed lineup with role hints and a track count per person. The Album Lineup panel surfaces a one-click "Use N from credits" CTA — and per-person "+ Name (3 tracks)" chips for cherry-picking — so the new lineup capability scales across the catalog without per-album hand-curation. Works for solo artists too: if their record's credits name session players, the panel appears with the proposal ready to accept.
- **Sub-brand vendors (one parent, many children).** A Gibson-owned brand like Epiphone or Kramer gets its own vendor row with its own domain, logo, bio, and instrument catalog — and a small "Owned by Gibson" line appears on the fan-side vendor sheet so listeners see the lineage without losing the sub-brand's identity. In the admin, pasting an Epiphone URL when Gibson already exists prompts "Add as a sub-brand of Gibson?" with one tap to confirm; the vendor's Overview adds a Lineage panel for picking or unlinking a parent and listing every sub-brand it owns. Sub-brands are one level deep — they can't own further sub-brands.
- **Makers + Resellers (split brands).** Every vendor in the catalog now carries two role flags: **Maker** (the brand that actually built the gear — Gibson, Steinway, Neumann) and **Reseller** (the storefront that sells it — Sweetwater, Reverb, Guitar Center). A row can be both: Gibson builds Les Pauls *and* sells them direct, so they appear on the Makers sidebar and the Resellers sidebar. The operator gets two dedicated index pages (`/admin/makers`, `/admin/vendors`) plus per-vendor toggles so promoting a reseller-only row into a Maker (or vice versa) takes one tap, never a delete-and-recreate.
- **Gear → Maker linkage.** Every piece of gear points at the single Maker that built it via a typeahead picker on its admin Overview, and the Maker name + logo now headline the gear on both the admin detail page and the fan-side gear page ("By Gibson" under the Les Paul title). Resellers stay on a dedicated Resellers tab; the fan-facing storefront list under each gear page is now labelled "Available at" so the page reads as **maker first, where-to-buy second**.
- **Gear.** The instruments themselves — guitars, basses, drums, mics, pedals — with photos, artist notes, the single Maker that built each piece, and the resellers that carry it. A Gear → People tab lets an operator pick a person, multi-select GoodTunes-release tracks (pre-filtered to ones that person already plays on, with an "All GoodTunes tracks" fallback) and credit them on this instrument in a single action — role is copied from their existing credit on each track when present.
- **Presses / Labels.** Record labels and vinyl pressing plants, with their own catalog of associated albums and print-run quotes. (Pressing plants used to be filed under "Manufacturers"; renamed so the word "Manufacturer" doesn't blur with instrument Makers.)
- **Pressing-plant & fulfillment partners.** Pressing plants and fulfillment warehouses are first-class entities in the admin — each with contact info, location, specialties (e.g. "180g black", "splatter"), standard turnaround time, and a default fulfillment partner per plant. Foundation for the in-app RFQ flow that lets GoodTunes invite multiple plants to bid on a print run.
- **Album bundle catalog.** Operators set per-album price, configure add-ons (vinyl variants, merch, signed copies), and map physical SKUs to the digital release — the same record powers both direct GoodTunes checkout and Shopify-bundle redemption. Physical formats now use a "+ Add physical good" picker (vinyl, double LP, 7", cassette, CD) instead of a fixed five-row grid, so the Sell panel only shows the formats the artist actually offers. Every format row now shows the same Price · Cost · Profit / Sold · Profit · Total two-column readout the signed-cert add-on uses, with an ⓘ tooltip breaking Cost into Manufacturing + Publishing + Payment processing + GoodTunes margin so artists can see exactly where the unit cost comes from before they price the run. For 7″ and 12″ vinyl rows the Manufacturing line is keyed off the inviting press's own **catalog** — each press configures the formats it runs, the color tiers it offers, the colors in each tier, and a quantity-keyed price ladder per tier. The artist picks tier → color, types a quantity, and the cost recomputes live as the quantity snaps up to the next ladder rung (with a "{rung}+ — request a custom quote" caveat above the cap). Hellbender ships pre-seeded so the existing 7″/12″ Black / Standard color / Regrind ladders work out of the box, and free / non-invited albums continue to use the published Hellbender rate sheet as a fallback. A small fan-preview thumbnail next to the pickers shows the album jacket with a colored disc peeking out so the artist can see what a customer will get. Picks are snapshotted on save. The Sell panel also opens with a Presses directory — info cards for every pressing plant GoodTunes works with (logo, location, turnaround, specialty chips) — and the footnote tells artists today's vinyl Cost defaults are Hellbender's reference rates. When a press invited an artist or label onto GoodTunes, that partner's Presses surface is hard-locked to just the inviting press until their first run actually ships, and the Sell-panel cost calculator prefers that press's per-format pricing (with the Hellbender matrix and platform defaults filling any gaps); once they ship a run the full directory unlocks, and super-admin can clear or reassign the invited press at any time from the partner's Identity panel.
- **Vendor-managed GoodDeed pricing portal.** Pressing plants, printers, and holographers each get their own sign-in (role=vendor) on a dedicated `/vendor` portal where they quote per-leg GoodDeed costs themselves — Printing on a 25/50/100/200/300/500 tier ladder, Hologram+shrinkwrap as a flat per-unit, and Insertion as a flat per-unit for vendors that also press. Each leg toggles Active/Draft independently. Inside an album's Sell panel the operator routes the run by picking one vendor per leg from a dropdown of vendors with active pricing for that service, and a live wholesale calculator recomputes the run's per-unit and total against the chosen run size. When the sale window closes the per-release pricing is snapshotted onto the addon so later vendor edits never rewrite history.
- **Signed-cert sale-window batch workflow.** Every signed-cert release runs on a defined sale window inside the album's Sell panel — operators set the open and close dates, fan orders that land in-window reserve a GoodDeed number, and a live counter shows progress against the 25-unit print minimum. At close (manual or auto-tick), GoodTunes either auto-refunds every signed-cert line if the window finished under 25 (fans keep the digital provenance page) or snapshots the vendor pricing onto the release and flips the batch into production. From there a single "Send to press" button bundles the in-batch certificates into a ZIP for the printer, and a six-step ops tracker (sent to printer → printed → signed → hologrammed → shrinkwrapped → inserted) walks the run to shipped with timestamps recorded on each step. Post-window orders fall back to digital-only automatically, and a true-up ledger row records the projected-vs-actual rung delta at close so the next batch's economics start from the same source of truth.
- **Signed-cert wholesale is published, tiered, and god-view editable.** GoodTunes's per-unit wholesale price for printed, signed, hologrammed GoodDeed certificates is a tiered ladder (default 25–49 = $13, 50–99 = $12, 100–199 = $9, 200–299 = $7, 300+ = $6) with a 25-unit minimum (smaller runs auto-refund) and billing on actual orders at window close. Super-admin can now edit each rung — add or remove rungs (up to 10), rename the batch label, and change the wholesale per unit — directly inside the admin Platform Pricing page; the first rung stays pinned to the 25-unit print floor so the auto-refund rule can't be edited around. The live ladder powers the Push-to-Shopify earnings preview and the future window-close auto-charge from the same source of truth, so operators always quote the same number the system bills. See [docs/shopify-pricing-strategy.md](./shopify-pricing-strategy.md#signed-cert-wholesale-ladder) for the full cost stack.
- **Platform pricing + artist profit readout.** A super-admin-only Platform Pricing page sets the wholesale cost of a printed & signed GoodDeed certificate and the per-order Shopify checkout fee. Every artist's signed-cert add-on shows a live "You earn $X.XX per unit" readout against that cost, in mint when they're profiting and in heart-pink when the price they've set would lose money. The platform cost is snapshot onto the add-on at save time, so raising the global cost never silently turns a saved artist add-on into a loss — they see the new number on their next save. Artists also pick a **planned quantity** for the signed run — either a fixed number (the Sell panel multiplies per-unit profit by the run to show a "Total if they all sell" number) or "as many as will sell" (Total shown as TBD). Fixed runs are a real soft cap on the fan-facing Buy sheet: once that many signed certificates have been bought across paid orders, the toggle disables with "All signed copies claimed" and the checkout endpoint rejects late attempts, so artists are never on the hook for more prints than they committed to.
- **Album engagement view.** Per-album orders, GoodDeed serials issued, and refund/void state, so operators can see at a glance how a release is performing.
- **Artist→GoodTunes pressing-order pipeline.** Every artist's Sell tab now opens with a five-stage progress strip — Select package → Upload art → Set price → Select quantity → **Go to Press!** — so the path from "I've made my choices" to "GoodTunes has an order to push to the press" is legible at a glance. The final "Go to Press!" button only lights up when every prior stage is green; clicking it snapshots the picked package (format, press, color, jacket, quantity, unit cost, total) and files it into a new admin **Pressing orders** review inbox (`/admin/pressing-orders`). GoodTunes ops sees pending requests with the album, artist, plant, quantity, total cost, and the live preflight status, and can Approve (marks ready-to-dispatch; press handoff is out-of-band for now) or Reject with a short note that surfaces back on the artist's Sell tab. Snapshot-based so later catalog edits never mutate a pending order.
- **Print-ready PDF generation per pressing plant.** Once an admin has settled on a vendor and a release's artwork is uploaded, one tap on the Sell panel composes the print PDFs that vendor actually wants — one file per template the release needs (center label, jacket or gatefold, optional insert) sized to that vendor's finished trim + bleed in points. Each file is named to the vendor's filename convention (`CATALOG#_ARTIST_TEMPLATE_yyyymmdd.pdf`, PMP's strictest regex), stored versioned in object storage, and listed back in the same panel so older generations stay downloadable. Per-template source mapping picks the right validated upload for each template (jacket vs. center label vs. insert); falling back to the album's primary artwork or proceeding past failing preflight checks both require a recorded justification.
- **Upload preflight against pressing-plant specs.** When an artist or label uploads art or audio for a vinyl run, GoodTunes validates the file against the strictest combined ruleset across our pressing plants (MRP, PMP, Hellbender) — finished + bleed size and PPI for the chosen template, color mode, embedded fonts, accepted file formats and filename conventions for art; required format (WAV) and bit depth, per-side length against the size + RPM table, one-file-per-side and side-break tracklist for audio. Pass / warn / fail rows render inline on the artist's Sell panel with the spec hints surfaced *before* the file picker, so they know the shape the bytes need to be. Results persist with the upload and surface as a per-order pill on the admin Orders queue; a fail blocks fulfillment until an admin writes a justification override (full audit trail kept).
- **Cross-section deep-link UX.** When an operator pivots from one entity to a related one — Gear to its Vendor, Person to their Gear — the destination remembers where they came from and offers a one-tap return crumb. Browsing the catalog feels like reading a wiki, not navigating a folder tree.
- **Paste-a-URL entity creation.** To add a new vendor, gear product, label, pressing-plant manufacturer, or non-profit partner, an operator pastes the company's website. GoodTunes scrapes the name, domain, logo, cover, bio, and (for plants) location automatically and warns if the entity is already in the catalog — onboarding a new partner takes seconds, and duplicates are caught at creation. Manufacturer, vendor, and label detail pages each carry a one-tap "Refresh from website" action that re-runs the same scrape against the saved URL and only overwrites scraped fields that came back non-null, so logos, covers, and bios stay current when a partner redesigns its site without clobbering hand-curated specialties or contact info.
- **NPO directory with contacts.** Non-profit partners are now first-class in the admin shell. From `/admin/non-profits`, an operator clicks **Add NPO**, pastes the org's main website, and GoodTunes pulls the name, logo, and homepage automatically — landing the operator on the NPO's detail page where they attach one or more People (with optional role labels like "Director" or "Program Lead") as contacts/reps. The same page surfaces the NPO's referral summary so operators can see attributed paid units and accrued $1/unit credits alongside who actually represents the org.
- **Shared contacts surface on every partner.** The same People-as-contacts panel is now wired into every partner type — vendors (makers + resellers), pressing plants, labels, fulfillment partners, and NPOs — so operators can record the buyer, A&R, production lead, or account rep on whichever org they're working with. Each panel ships with a "Paste LinkedIn" add-flow that reads the public profile (name, headline, avatar), rehosts the avatar, and find-or-creates the underlying Person matched by their canonical LinkedIn URL — a single paste turns into a saved contact, with a graceful fallback when LinkedIn blocks the scrape so the link still gets captured.
- **Grid / list toggle.** Every admin index page (Albums, People, Gear, Vendors, Labels) carries an Apple-Music-style segmented control to switch between a visual grid and a scannable list. Preference persists per section.
- **Light admin theme.** The operator surface uses a clean white-and-slate Mac-app skin, distinct from the dark fan player — same brand vocabulary, denser layout for getting work done.
- **2FA-protected admin sign-in.** Every admin sign-in requires a second factor — by default a one-time code emailed to the admin, with an option to switch to an authenticator-app TOTP enrollment and scrypt-hashed recovery codes. Super-admin grant and revoke are gated to existing super-admins from inside the app. Admins can also rotate their own password from `/admin/security` — current password as proof, 8-character minimum, no DB ticket required (OAuth-only admins see the card in a disabled state with a short explanation). A **Forgot password?** link on `/admin/login` emails a single-use, 30-minute reset link that lets a locked-out admin pick a new password without operator help — the 2FA gate still fires before they reach the admin shell, so a stolen reset email alone isn't enough. Fans signing into the player at `/login` get the same self-serve recovery — a **Forgot password?** link on the dark customer chrome emails a single-use, 30-minute reset link against `customer_users`, OAuth-only fans (Google/Apple) are silently skipped, and the same non-enumerating neutral-200 + per-IP/per-email rate limit guarantees apply, so locked-out fans no longer have to email support.
- **Admin invitations.** A super-admin can invite a teammate by email and pre-assign their role (super admin, label, artist, manufacturer, or fulfillment partner). Scoped roles bind to a specific entity at invite time — picking "Artist" surfaces a searchable Person picker, "Label" surfaces a Label picker, and so on — so the invitee logs in already wired to the right artist or label and sees their own dashboard data on the first page. The invitee gets a one-shot link, sets a username and password, and lands directly in the admin shell with the right scope baked in — no shared credentials, no manual database edits. Invite emails ship from a verified GoodTunes sender (`noreply@send.goodtunes.music`, DKIM + SPF authenticated) so they reach any inbox — not just Bill's — and arrive without "via resend.dev" attribution. The pending-invites list shows each invite's scoped entity (name + thumbnail) so Bill can see at a glance who each link is for, and pending invites can be revoked from the same screen.
- **Artist reporting dashboard.** A Stripe-grade `/artist` view scoped to each artist account: KPI strip (gross, artist share, units, plays, unique listeners, top track, top album, completion rate) with last-period comparison, daily revenue and plays charts, a buyers-and-listeners-by-country panel, top-tracks and top-albums tables, a reconciles-to-Stripe orders log, and an Audience tab with new-vs-returning, a repeat-listener cohort, and top fans. Every table exports to CSV. Scope honors primary-artist, payout-owner, and credited-performer/writer rules so labels see only their roster. When an artist was brought on by a specific press, the dashboard header carries a quiet read-only "Invited by {Press}" credit (logo + a link to message GoodTunes if they need to switch) that softens to "Originally invited by …" once their first physical run ships — so the relationship is legible without giving the partner an unlock button.
- **Label rollup reporting dashboard.** A Stripe-grade `/label` view scoped to each label account: roster-wide KPIs (gross revenue, units, buyers, plays, listeners, new fans, completion rate, average revenue per artist) with last-period comparison, a stacked revenue-by-artist chart across the roster, daily revenue and plays, a buyers-and-listeners-by-country panel, top-albums and top-tracks tables, and a recent-orders log. The headline view is a sortable roster table — one row per artist with revenue, units, buyers, plays, and listeners — and every artist row drills straight into that artist's `/artist` dashboard with the same date range carried through. Every table exports to CSV; scope is enforced server-side so a label only sees its own roster and an artist's drill-through page only loads if they're on that label. Same "Invited by {Press}" credit as the artist dashboard appears in the header when a press brought the label onto GoodTunes, softening once the label has shipped any physical run.
- **Product-analytics pipeline.** Every meaningful fan interaction — plays, favorites, playlist edits, searches, gear-vendor taps, shares, checkouts, sign-ins — flows through a typed event registry, gets enriched with device, session, user, platform and geo, batches to our own Postgres event store, and forwards server-side to PostHog. Admins get a live in-app debug overlay on every page to confirm any new event is wired end-to-end. Foundation for LCID and every future artist/label dashboard.
- **Partner reporting.** Labels, artists, NPO referrers, and person referrers each see their own Reports section inside the admin shell — sales over time, units sold, plays & GoodSync engagement, payouts received, Shopify redemption rate, top fans by spend (name + city only, never email or address), and a world fan map. Every report respects a global date-range picker and exports to CSV in one click. Super-admins can scope-in as any partner via a "view as partner" bar to see exactly what that label or artist sees.
- **Super-admin god-view reporting.** Beyond the partner cuts, GoodTunes operators get a platform-wide reporting layer on the same `/admin/reports` page — headline KPIs (GMV, net platform revenue, AOV, unique buyers, new signups, DAU/WAU/MAU, visit-to-first-purchase conversion, refund rate), revenue broken down by SKU kind, label, artist, and country, plays-based top-tracks/albums/artists/labels leaderboards, embedded PostHog funnel + cohort dashboards (via configurable iframe URLs), an ops-health surface flagging stuck OrderDesk fulfillments and abandoned checkouts, a per-owner payout reconciliation table that compares computed-at-ship payouts against actual Stripe Connect transfers (with deltas surfaced for skipped/failed transfers), and a raw analytics-event explorer with name/user/session filters. Every cut exports to CSV.
- **Admin dashboard home.** `/admin` now lands on a Stripe-style overview — KPI tiles for Gross sales, Net revenue, Orders, New fans, and Plays (with deltas vs the prior period and inline sparklines), a Today/7d/30d/90d/All time-range switcher that persists across reloads, a primary trend chart (toggleable across GMV, Orders, and New fans) with the prior period drawn as a faint comparison line, a Recent activity rail merging orders, signups, and payouts with deep links into the matching admin pages, and an Ops-health strip that surfaces stuck fulfillments / failed checkouts / stuck payouts (and hides itself when nothing is wrong). Albums stays one click away in the sidebar.
- **Customer directory.** A read-only Customers section inside the admin shell lists every fan account with a server-side roll-up — order count, lifetime spend (paid + shipped only), and last activity — searchable by name, email, or username. Each row drills into a full profile: contact details, shipping and billing addresses, every order (with status pill + Good Deed serial), the fan's collection of owned albums (cert # + acquisition date), and their playlists. When a Stripe customer is linked, a Stripe button deep-links into the live Stripe dashboard. Cross-linked from the Orders index so an operator can pivot from "who bought this?" to "what else have they bought?" in one tap.
- **Per-partner permissions + post-sale edit lock.** Every label or artist partner has five per-scope permission toggles — edit metadata, upload masters, map Shopify, manage payouts, invite sub-users — set by GoodTunes from the partner's admin page. An optional "edits require approval" mode routes any metadata change into a pending-changes queue at `/admin/review`, where GoodTunes can approve, reject, or approve-with-edits (tweaking the diff before it lands) with full audit trail. Once an album records its first paid sale it locks against partner metadata edits to protect what fans have already bought; super-admins can grant a single-edit or 24-hour unlock with a recorded reason. Partners with the invite-sub-users permission can grow their own team from inside the admin shell — invites are force-pinned to the inviter's role and scope, so a label can add label-side teammates but never escalate or cross-scope.
- **Referral economics.** Artists referred onto GoodTunes carry a referrer chain — either a non-profit that brought them in, another artist who introduced them (Fernando-style), or both. Each referrer earns a flat $1.00 per paying unit by default (adjustable per artist), and sees a Referrals report in their own admin shell with the units and earnings rolled up by referred artist.

## Integrations

The third-party services GoodTunes already runs against in production.

- **Replit Object Storage.** Durable cloud storage for every operator-uploaded asset — album art, person photos, vendor logos and covers, scraped instrument images — served through stable public URLs that survive redeploys.
- **OpenAI.** Powers operator-facing AI tools across the admin — biography drafting, content suggestion, structured metadata extraction, and SuperCredits™ enrichment — so the catalog can be built quickly with the artist's voice intact.
- **ElevenLabs.** Voice-AI engine behind GoodTunes' lyric workflows — transcribes vocals from a master and aligns artist-supplied lyrics word-by-word, then diffs the two so the artist can confirm or correct any mismatches before publishing. Synced lyrics ship without weeks of manual timing.
- **Mux.** Encrypted, adaptive-bitrate streaming with per-session signed tokens — masters never leave our infrastructure as a downloadable file. Same delivery model used by HBO Max and Robinhood. New uploads ingest into Mux automatically, and a startup sweep brings any pre-Mux masters into the pipeline.
- **Spotify.** Used at the catalog level to enrich albums, artists, and tracks with the canonical metadata fans recognize — release dates, artwork, identifiers — without depending on Spotify for playback.
- **GitHub.** Source of truth for every line of GoodTunes code, with automated checks on every change — engineering velocity stays high while production stays safe.
- **OrderDesk.** Every paid physical order — vinyl, cassette, CD, bundled merch, whether bought directly through GoodTunes or bundled into a label's Shopify checkout — is handed off to OrderDesk the moment Stripe (or Shopify) confirms payment, with the album, artist, label, and SKU stamped on the carton so the right warehouse sees the right routing. OrderDesk signs status webhooks back to GoodTunes (submitted → in fulfillment → shipped → delivered, plus carrier and tracking number/URL), and operators see the full lifecycle on the admin order row with a one-click retry if a handoff ever fails.
- **Google Sign-In.** Fans and admins can sign in with their Google account — one tap, no password.
- **Apple Sign-In.** Apple ID is a first-class login option for fans on iOS-heavy devices. Fans land back in the player whether they picked "Share My Email" or "Hide My Email"; identity is anchored on Apple's stable subject claim (never the email), so the same fan always resolves to the same account on resign-in. When Apple returns a private-relay forwarder, a banner in Account prompts the fan to verify a real deliverable address — once they do, a later Google sign-in with that same real email lands in the same account instead of creating a duplicate. Apple's domain-verification file (`/.well-known/apple-developer-domain-association.txt`) is served from disk on both `my.` and `admin.` hosts so it survives redeploys.
- **Stripe.** Direct in-player checkout for album bundles (digital + optional vinyl, merch, add-ons) runs on Stripe Embedded Checkout. Webhook-driven order fulfillment unlocks the album the instant payment clears, assigns the buyer's GoodDeed serial number, and reverses cleanly on refund. Stripe Connect powers artist and label payouts when an order is marked fulfilled.
- **Shopify Bundle.** Labels and artists who already sell physical product on Shopify can bundle GoodTunes digital access into the same checkout. After the buyer pays on the label's store, our app unlocks the album for the email on the order, mints a one-time redemption code, and adds a "Get your music now" button to both the order-status page and the confirmation email. The fan lands on a branded redeem page that already knows their name, sets a password (or signs in if they already have GoodTunes), and drops straight into the player with their GoodDeed number assigned. Refunds reverse the unlock automatically. Operators connect a label's store from our admin in two clicks — no plugin to download — and each album's admin Shopify tab maps the physical product (or specific variant) to the digital release. For labels who don't yet have a Shopify product, the admin Shopify tab also offers a one-click "Push to Shopify as a draft" — it creates the product in the label's store as a draft (never auto-published) with a "GoodTunes Edition" variant priced at the bundle price and inventory-capped to the album's redemption cap, plus an optional "+ Signed printed GoodDeed" variant when the signed-cert add-on is enabled, with a live earnings preview against the published signed-cert wholesale ladder. Re-pushing detects label-side edits in Shopify (title, body, variant prices/inventory) and asks the operator to confirm before overwriting. Once an album is pushed, the Shopify tab also shows a **Live on Shopify** mirror — current retail price and lifetime units sold per variant (GoodTunes Edition + signed-cert, side-by-side when both exist), pulled live from the Shopify Admin API with a one-click "Refresh from Shopify" and a relative-time stamp so operators can answer "what's it priced at, how many moved?" without leaving the album panel. Variants deleted on Shopify show "Removed in Shopify" instead of erroring.
